Booster K1 Humanoid Robot — 10-Week Course

Welcome to the Booster K1 Humanoid Robot course. Over 10 weeks you will go from first power-on to building fully autonomous behaviors combining voice control, computer vision, and reinforcement learning on a 22-DOF humanoid platform.

Course Equipment
  • Booster K1 Humanoid Robot (22 DOF, Jetson Orin NX 16 GB)
  • Laptop / workstation running Ubuntu 22.04 on the same Ethernet subnet
  • SSH client and a ROS 2 Humble installation
